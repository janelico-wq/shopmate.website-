About ShopMate

ShopMate is an online grocery shopping and personal shopping service concept designed to help busy students, workers, and families save time when buying everyday essentials. Customers can browse products, plan their budget, and prepare a shopping list for delivery or pickup.

Product graphics and price note: Every product card displays a custom illustrative graphic generated for this ShopMate prototype. These graphics help identify the product type but are not official brand package photos. Prices are reference prices from the public Shop Gaisano online catalog, not guaranteed shelf prices for every branch. Prices and stock can change; check the linked Gaisano catalog for current details.
